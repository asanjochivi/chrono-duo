## Fixed

- Nintendo DS save import now keeps story progress and position. The importer read the DS event flags one byte out of place, so mid-game DS saves loaded at the start of the game and showed "The Millennial Fair" on the load screen, even though characters, levels and items came through correctly. Imported DS saves now show the right chapter and resume on the map where they were saved. Re-import any DS save you brought over with v0.6.

## Notes

- Install over your previous build; don't uninstall first or you'll lose local ChronoDuo settings and saves not backed up elsewhere.
- Still requires the official CHRONO TRIGGER (Upgrade Ver.) installed from Google Play. ChronoDuo redistributes none of Square Enix's code or assets.
- Known limitation: RAR5, gzip, and tar-packaged mods aren't supported yet — only zip, 7z, and RAR4.
